Design Constraints & Architectural Guarantees

  • 🛡️ Zero Browsing History Upload: URL reputation is resolved locally using deterministic k-anonymity prefixes and local blocklists. Full URLs never leave your machine.
  • 🔒 No Screen Recording: Screen inspection is strictly confined to transient heuristic matching during active remote-session events and discarded instantly.
  • 🚫 Zero Third-Party Telemetry: No tracking SDKs, no behavioral advertising hooks, and no sale of telemetry to data brokers.
  • ⚙️ Right to Erasure & Portability: All threat logs and quarantine records can be exported or purged instantly by the user.
